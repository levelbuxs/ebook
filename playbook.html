Step one

Pick the identity

Lock this before you shoot anything — the @handle goes in the corner of every proof photo and every video, so picking later means reshoots. Click any card to shortlist it; it copies the whole set (brand, handle, hashtag) to your clipboard.

My pick is The Walmart Glitch / @walmartglitch, and the reason is simple: it names the retailer (which people are searching for) and frames it as a "glitch" (which is what people think it is). It's direct, memorable, and SEO-friendly.

How to choose in 60 seconds: say each name out loud as if you're telling a friend at a party. If you have to explain it, cut it. Then check the handle is free on TikTok and Instagram before you fall in love with it — the handle matters more than the domain now.

Step two

Section starter text

The first line of every section decides whether the rest of it gets read. Each block below is the job of that section, then three openers you can steal. Brackets are fill-in-the-blanks.

The hero / above the fold

Job: create disbelief + curiosity in under 3 seconds. Mention the AI assistant.

A — The big number opener (strongest)

I've received over $[10,000] worth of merchandise from Walmart and paid $0.00 for all of it. This isn't about coupons or cashback. It's about activating a hidden system inside Walmart's own platform. And I'm not doing it manually — I use a powerful AI assistant to automate 90% of the work. You get free access to it when you buy this guide.

Leads with a number and a claim, then immediately offers the AI solution. Use this one.

B — The story opener

Three months ago, I was sleeping on an air mattress. Last week, a $200 memory foam mattress was delivered to my door. The charge? $0.00. Welcome to the Walmart Glitch.

Story-based, very relatable. Great for social media captions.

C — The SEO opener

If you've ever searched "how to get free stuff from Walmart," you've probably run into scams, outdated methods, or half-truths. This is different. This is the real Walmart reviewer program — and I'm going to show you exactly how to get in.

Targets people already searching for this. Works well for Google Ads.
The proof section

Job: shut down "pics or it didn't happen" before they think it.

A

$10,000+ in free merchandise. Not samples. Not travel-size. Full-size, full-price items delivered to my door with a receipt that says $0.00.

Short, punchy, specific.

B

Every one of these has a photo and a video. And my @handle in frame, so you can check it's actually me.

The handle-in-frame detail kills the "stolen photo" objection.

C

Here's the part people don't believe: the $349 TV, the $200 mattress, the $549 iPad. All for zero. All in my house right now.

Names the specific items that create disbelief.
The method section

Job: reframe the failure as mechanical, not personal. Introduce the AI.

A

Walmart runs a legitimate reviewer program that rewards customers who consistently purchase and review items. The "glitch" is that almost nobody knows how to trigger it — or that it even exists.

Positions it as real and legitimate.

B

It's not luck. It's an application problem. Most people try once, get rejected, and decide they're not eligible. They are — they just didn't set the account up right.

Reframes failure as a mechanical issue, not a personal one.

C

There are three paths: aged account (fastest), new account (2-3 months), or Walmart+ member (boost). Chapter 2 covers all three. And yes, I use an AI to automate the review writing — you get free access to it.

Teases the three paths and mentions the AI gift.
The AI assistant section

Job: sell the AI as the unique value proposition.

A

How can you possibly write 100 detailed, funny, 1000-character reviews? You don't. You let an AI do it for you. And I'm giving you free access to the platform I use.

Direct and benefit-focused.

B

Go to [AI Platform], take your item, write two simple lines about it, and use the prompt I provide. The AI instantly generates the perfect review. Copy, paste, add your photos, submit. You've just done 30 minutes of work in 30 seconds.

Explains the process, making it feel easy.

C

Your free gift: 1 year of unlimited AI access (no caps, no limits). Use it for reviews, content, or anything else. The same platform I use to automate 90% of the work.

Highlights the value of the bonus.
The metrics section

Job: make the requirements feel achievable.

A

The numbers that matter: 100+ approved reviews, 250k+ total views, 25–75+ thumbs up. Once you hit these, you're about to unlock the glitch.

Clear, specific, actionable.

B

For aged accounts with review history: you might already be close. For new accounts: 2–3 months of consistency gets you there. Chapter 5 has the exact timeline.

Reassures both account types.

C

It's not about luck or timing. It's about hitting specific metrics that trigger the system. Follow the method, hit the numbers, apply, and get approved.

Reinforces that it's a system, not luck.
The inside/TOC section

Job: make the guide feel complete and actionable.

A

The full method, the exact metrics, and the direct application link. Short on purpose — you should be able to start tonight.

Emphasizes speed and completeness.

B

Chapter 2 covers the three paths: Veteran (easiest), Newcomer (2–3 months), Pro (Walmart+ boost). Chapter 3 covers the Perfect Review method. Chapter 4 gives you free AI access.

Teases specific chapters to create curiosity.

C

No fluff chapters about mindset. Chapter 1 gives you my story and the proof. Chapter 2 tells you which path to take. Chapter 3 shows you how to execute.

Promises no filler — the #1 fear when buying any guide.
The price section

Job: make the price feel small next to the value. Mention the AI.

A

$29.99. That's less than the last thing you bought on Amazon and forgot about. One $349 TV covers this 11 times over. Plus you get free AI access worth $200+.

Concrete comparison + bonus value.

B

I priced it at $29.99 because that's below the point where people need to think about it. And honestly, with the free AI access, it's basically free anyway.

Honest pricing rationale + AI bonus framing.

C

It's $29.99 today. The AI access alone is worth more than that. Consider it a no-brainer.

Simple and direct.
The close

Job: one last small push. Mention the AI.

A

You'll spend more than $29.99 on something forgettable this month. An hour from now you could have the application submitted. Or you could keep scrolling. Both are fine — only one of them ends with a free TV in your living room.

Gives them an out, which paradoxically raises conversion.

B

Worst case you're out $29.99 and you know the method — plus you get free AI access. Best case a $349 TV shows up next week. Those are the actual two outcomes.

Frames risk as tiny by naming both outcomes plainly.

C

If it doesn't work, email me, I'll send your money back, and you keep the guide and the AI access. I can offer that because almost nobody asks.

The most honest version of a guarantee — and it converts better.

"I've received over $10,000 worth of stuff from Walmart and paid $0.00."

"Last week a $349 TV showed up at my door. I paid nothing."

"Three months ago I was on an air mattress. Now I'm on a $200 memory foam."

"Stop clipping coupons. The version where the total is literally zero is easier."

"I'm not saying 'free stuff' like a scammer — I'm showing you a receipt."

"There's a hidden system inside Walmart. I found it."

"The secret? 3-5 photos, 700 characters, and a funny first line."

Step four

Full ad copy

Three lengths. Same offer, same structure: hook → proof → method → AI reveal → ask.

Short (TikTok / Reels / Stories · ~15 sec)

"I've received over $10,000 worth of stuff from Walmart and paid $0.00 for all of it.

It's not a glitch or exploit — it's a legitimate reviewer program that almost nobody knows how to trigger.

I wrote it all down. It's $29.99 and comes with free AI access. Link's right there."

On-screen text idea: put "$0.00" on screen the entire time, small, in the corner.
Medium (Facebook / IG feed · 60–90 words)

"Last month a $349 4K TV, a $200 memory foam mattress, and a $549 iPad showed up at my house and the receipt said $0.00.

It's not coupons, it's not cashback, and it's not a glitch that'll get patched next week. It's Walmart's legitimate reviewer program — you just have to know how to get approved and where to look.

The method is simple but tedious. That's why I use an AI assistant to automate 90% of it. And you get free, unlimited access to it when you buy this guide.

$29.99, instant download, refund if you're not approved within 30 days."

The AI reveal is the hook that separates you from every other "free stuff" guide.
Long (email / blog / sales page body)

"I need to open by saying this sounds like a scam, and I get it. If I saw this ad I'd scroll past too.

So here's the receipt instead of a pitch: last Thursday, a $349 4K TV. Total paid: $0.00. The week before, a $200 memory foam mattress. Same total. The week before that, a $549 iPad.

Here's what it isn't. It isn't coupons — I don't clip anything. It isn't cashback — I'm not waiting for 3% to dribble into an account. It isn't a glitch somebody found and posted to Reddit that'll be dead by Friday.

What it actually is: Walmart's official reviewer program. They don't advertise it, because the moment a hundred thousand people apply in the same week, the good items dry up.

But — and this is the whole thing — there's an order to getting approved. You need to hit specific metrics: 100+ reviews, 250k+ views, 25–75+ thumbs up. That sounds like a lot of work, and it is — if you're doing it manually.

That's where the AI comes in. I use a powerful AI assistant to write perfect reviews in 30 seconds. And as a buyer, you get free, unlimited access to the same platform. That's a $200+ value, and it's yours just for buying the guide.

I wrote the whole method down, including the direct application link, the exact metrics, and the mistakes that get people rejected. It's 54 pages, mostly screenshots.

$29.99. If you follow chapters 2 through 5, apply, and aren't approved within 30 days, email me and I'll refund you — and you keep the guide and the AI access."

The AI paragraph is the highest-leverage paragraph in the whole ad. It's your unique selling proposition.
