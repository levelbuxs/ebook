## Rules I'd Actually Follow

1. **Specific beats impressive.** "A $349 4K TV" beats "expensive electronics."
2. **Name the objection first.** If you don't say "this sounds like a scam," they say it to themselves anyway.
3. **Never cut the video during the claim.** The boring parts are what make it believable.
4. **The "here's what it isn't" paragraph is the highest-leverage paragraph in the ad.** Never cut it.
5. **One fake screenshot ends it.** Don't ship a claim you can't back up.
6. **Fake urgency costs more than it makes.** No countdown timers unless the deadline is real.
7. **Keep the "skip this if" column.** Turning away bad-fit buyers drops refunds and chargebacks to near zero.
8. **Lead with the AI.** It's your unique selling proposition — the thing nobody else is offering.
9. **Use the retailer name.** "Walmart Glitch" is more searchable than "Free Stuff Method." People are already searching for it.
10. **Make it about the method, not the free stuff.** You're selling the *how*, not the *what*.
