Before you ask

Questions people actually DM me

Why won't you just tell me which store it is?

Because if I put the name on a public page, ten thousand people apply in the same week, the approval criteria tighten, and the good items stop showing up for the people already in. It's a real program the retailer runs on purpose — I'm just not going to be the reason it gets flooded. The name and the application link are on page 1 of the guide. If that arrangement bothers you, honestly, don't buy it.

Is this a loophole? Could I get in trouble?

No. It's an official reviewer program. The retailer wants reviews from real customers and gives items away to get them. You're not exploiting anything — you're doing exactly what the program was built for. The reason it feels secret is only that they don't advertise it.

What's the catch?

You have to write honest reviews of what you receive. That's the deal, and it's what makes the whole thing legitimate. Reviews take about five minutes. If you stop writing them, you lose access — chapter 8 covers exactly how to stay in good standing.

Do I need an old account?

No, but it changes your odds and your timeline. A brand-new account with no review history and a 12-month-old account with history get processed differently. Chapter 2 covers both routes, including which one is actually faster — it's not the one you'd guess.

I have zero reviews. Am I just out?

No. You'll take the slower path and there's some setup work first, but it's very doable. Chapter 3 is entirely about building enough review history to clear the bar, and it's the shortest chapter in the book.

How fast can I realistically get approved?

It varies by account, and I give honest ranges in chapter 5 rather than a number that sounds good. Some people are in within days. Some wait a few weeks. Either way you'll know where you stand, and chapter 5 covers what to do if you hear nothing.

Can I pick what I get?

You pick from whatever is on the list at the time — you don't get to request a specific model. But the list includes genuinely big things: mattresses, e-bikes, wine fridges, podcast gear. Chapter 6 is about catching those before they're gone.

Does shipping cost anything?

No. That's part of why this isn't a discount — the total is zero, including delivery.

Why only $29.99? That seems cheap for this.

Because a high price gets people to screenshot it and post it publicly, which is exactly what I'm trying to avoid. $29.99 is below the point where somebody bothers to share it. That's the actual reason.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Follow chapters 2 through 4, apply, and if you're not approved within 30 days, email me and I'll refund you and you keep the guide. Most of the time when someone tells me it didn't work, it turns out they skipped a setup step — chapter 7 lists all six rejection reasons.

Is this only in the US?

Yes, as far as I know. I've only ever done it with a US account and US address. If you're outside the US, the guarantee covers you — try it and get a refund if it doesn't apply to your region.
